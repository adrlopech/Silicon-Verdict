[Wikipedia: Grand Theft Auto VI]
Following two delays, further leaks, and an extensive marketing campaign, it is scheduled to be released on 19 November 2026 for the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
The game will also be playable on Xbox One via Xbox Cloud Gaming.
Whilst this content would be locked behind the Ultimate Edition, Rockstar confirmed to Kinda Funny that there would be no microtransactions in either version of the game at launch.
Additionally, Rockstar also confirmed that there is no generative AI in the game.
Physical versions, containing download codes in lieu of discs to prevent leaks and reselling, are set to be available in shops on 12 November, allowing pre-loading a week before the game unlocks.
Rather than the unlimited weapon inventory system from previous series entries, Grand Theft Auto VI restricts the player to carrying two primary firearms and two sidearms at once.
Weapons are stored and transported in the player's vehicle, much like weapons being stored on the player's horse in Red Dead Redemption 2.
Rockstar avoided the use of procedural generation for creating NPCs in Grand Theft Auto VI, opting to handcraft NPCs who have their own personality traits determined by what district they are in, what they have seen, and how the player has behaved towards them.
Grand Theft Auto VI is set within the fictional US state of Leonida, based on Florida; its map is reported to be three times larger than the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2.
Throughout the game, players will be able to gain or lose weight as both Jason and Lucia, and if the player stays awake as either character for days on end, it will become visible on their faces.
