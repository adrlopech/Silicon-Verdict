[Wikipedia: Grand Theft Auto VI]
Grand Theft Auto VI is the eighth main entry in the Grand Theft Auto series, following 2013's Grand Theft Auto V.
Principal production started after the launch of Red Dead Redemption 2 in 2018.
Following two delays, further leaks, and an extensive marketing campaign, it is scheduled to be released on 19 November 2026 for the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
It is Rockstar's first game specifically built for ninth-generation consoles after porting Grand Theft Auto V to the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S in 2022.
Grand Theft Auto VI has over 600,000 unique NPC animations, up from 300,000 in Red Dead Redemption 2 and 55,000 in Grand Theft Auto V.
When Red Dead Redemption 2 released, Rockstar had 2,441 employees, which grew to 4,711 across 12 offices during the development of Grand Theft Auto VI.
Rockstar had previously implemented a volumetric clouds and fog system in Red Dead Redemption 2 while Grand Theft Auto VI brings more realistic cloud formations that dynamically move across the game world to emulate the variety of weather conditions in Florida and its tropical climate.
Grand Theft Auto VI's weather system was re-engineered from Red Dead Redemption 2 in order to achieve what Owen Shepherd, Rockstar vice president of art for lighting and rendering, called a "much more localised and persistent" world that "achieve[s] a sense of scale we haven't felt before".
Grand Theft Auto VI is set within the fictional US state of Leonida, based on Florida; its map is reported to be three times larger than the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2.
In order to help undertake such a workload, Rockstar Los Angeles was founded in 2023 as other Rockstar Games studios did not have the required production capabilities.
Principal production began in 2020, code-named Project Americas.
Grand Theft Auto VI was formally revealed in December 2023.
The trailer broke some YouTube records within the first day, and surpassed the lifetime viewership of Grand Theft Auto V's 2011 reveal trailer within two days, with 101 million views.
A second trailer was released on 6 May 2025, alongside a website update with 70 screenshots and several character and location descriptions.
It received a record 475 million views across all platforms within 24 hours, and won Best Game Trailer at the Golden Joystick Awards in 2025.
Following years of speculation and anticipation, Rockstar confirmed in February 2022 that the game was in development.
