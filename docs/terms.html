Acceptance of these terms

These terms apply to this website and to the Silicon Verdict channels: YouTube @SiliconVerdictHQ and TikTok @siliconverdict (together, "Silicon Verdict"). By reading the site or watching the videos you accept them. If you do not, please do not use them.

02

What Silicon Verdict is

Silicon Verdict is a one-person studio that makes short vertical videos (just over a minute) about PC hardware, gaming technology and everyday computing. Most of the work is automated by software the channel owner wrote and runs on their own computer:

  • topics are picked from the channel's own viewing statistics;
  • scripts are drafted by AI language models (Google Gemini, Cloudflare Workers AI) and checked by an automated fact-check pass before anything is rendered;
  • the narration is a synthetic voice made with ElevenLabs, and the video is drawn with Remotion;
  • a technical check (length, format, audio) runs on every video before it is scheduled.

No accounts, no service for others. Silicon Verdict is not an app for the public: there is no sign-up, no user account, nothing to buy and no service offered to third parties. The software only publishes to the channel's own accounts.

03

The videos are information, not advice

The videos explain how technology works and give the channel's opinion. Please read them that way:

  • Prices change. Prices shown in "worth it today" videos are the lowest listing found on a public Spanish shop (PcComponentes) on the date in the description. They may be different when you watch.
  • Rankings are opinions. Tier letters (S to F) are the channel's editorial judgement on a stated criterion, such as value for gaming. They are not a test result.
  • Mistakes can happen. Scripts are AI-assisted and checked automatically, but no check is perfect. If you spot an error, write to us and we will correct or remove the video.
  • Not professional advice. Nothing in the videos is financial, purchasing, legal or safety advice. Check the manufacturer's information before buying or changing hardware or settings.
