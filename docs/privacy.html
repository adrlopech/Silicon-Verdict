01

Who we are and what this covers

This policy explains what data Silicon Verdict handles: this website, the YouTube channel @SiliconVerdictHQ, the TikTok account @siliconverdict, and the software that produces and publishes the videos. Silicon Verdict is run by one person, who is responsible for the data described here. Contact: imnotchego@gmail.com.

In short: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics or ad trackers, nothing sold. The only personal data we handle is the public comments people leave on our own YouTube videos, and only to answer them.

02

Visitors to this website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools. It loads no scripts, fonts or images from other companies: every file, including the video covers, is served from this site.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address in its server logs to run and protect the service, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs.
  • Links. Links to YouTube, TikTok and other sites take you to those sites, where their own privacy policies apply.
03

Viewers of the videos

YouTube and TikTok handle your viewing data under their own policies (Google Privacy Policy, TikTok Privacy Policy). From YouTube we only receive the channel's own statistics through the YouTube Analytics API: views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers gained and traffic sources, as totals per video and per day. These numbers do not identify any viewer. They are used only to decide which kinds of videos to make next.

04

Comments on our YouTube videos

To answer comments, the software reads the public comments on the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API and lists the ones that have no reply yet.

  • What is used: the comment text, the commenter's public display name, the video and the date.
  • Suggested replies: to draft a reply, the comment text (up to 500 characters) and the video title, without your name, are sent to an AI model: Cloudflare Workers AI, or Google Gemini if that fails.
  • A person decides: the owner reads every suggestion and approves, edits or drops it. Nothing is posted automatically.
  • Where it is kept: a working list of unanswered comments is kept only on the owner's computer. It is not shared, sold or used for anything else, and you can ask for it to be deleted.
05

Google and YouTube API data

Silicon Verdict signs in only to its own Google account and channel, with these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: upload and schedule the channel's videos and thumbnails;
  • youtube.readonly: read the channel's own videos and playlists;
  • youtube.force-ssl: read comments on the channel's own videos and post replies the owner approved;
  • yt-analytics.readonly: read the channel's own statistics.

Silicon Verdict's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This data is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models.

The software also uses the YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.

06

TikTok

Silicon Verdict has a registered TikTok developer app that may only publish videos to its own account, @siliconverdict. At present TikTok videos are uploaded by the owner in TikTok Studio. Silicon Verdict does not read TikTok users' profiles, followers, messages or viewing data.

07

Services used to make the videos

These services receive only video material, never data about visitors or viewers:

  • Google Gemini and Cloudflare Workers AI: topics and script text, to draft and fact-check scripts;
  • ElevenLabs: the narration text, to produce the voice;
  • Public sources: product prices from public shop listings, and official game screenshots and trailers from Steam store pages.
08

Keys and security

Sign-in tokens and API keys are stored only on the owner's computer, in local files that are excluded from the public code repository. All calls to Google, YouTube, TikTok and the other services use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

09

How long data is kept, and your choices

  • Comments: the working list is kept only as long as it is useful to answer comments, and deleted on request.
  • Statistics: the channel's totals are kept as reports on the owner's computer. They contain no personal data.
  • Your rights: you can ask what data about you we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its use, by writing to imnotchego@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Removing your comment: you can delete your own comment on YouTube at any time; it then stops appearing to our software too.
  • Revoking access: anyone who connected a Google account to this software can remove its access on the Google account permissions page, and on TikTok in Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. Only the channel's own accounts are ever connected.
10

Children

The videos are made for a general audience interested in technology. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
