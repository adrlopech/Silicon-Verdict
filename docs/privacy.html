01

Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Silicon Verdict ("we", "us", or "our") handles data in connection with our website, telemetry rendering engines, and third-party developer integrations (such as the TikTok Content Posting API and YouTube Data API).

Core Privacy Guarantee: Silicon Verdict is an automated hardware benchmark analytics tool. We do not collect, track, store, profile, sell, or rent any personal information, cookies, or telemetry from visitors or third-party users.

02

Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike commercial data platforms, our application is designed with zero data collection principles:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for, capture, or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • No Browsing Telemetry or Tracking Cookies: We do not deploy third-party analytics trackers (e.g., Google Analytics, Meta Pixel) or tracking cookies on this static documentation portal.
  • No Financial or Payment Information: Silicon Verdict is completely free of billing systems and does not collect payment details.
03

Third-Party API Data & TikTok Developer Policies

Silicon Verdict accesses the TikTok Content Posting API v2 and YouTube Data API v3 strictly for creator publishing automation. In accordance with the TikTok Developer Terms and Privacy Policies:

  • Limited API Scope: The application requests access solely to publish original video content (video files, descriptions, and hashtags) directly to the authorized Silicon Verdict channel (@siliconverdict).
  • No Follower or Public User Data Harvested: Silicon Verdict does not read, aggregate, or process profile information, viewing habits, direct messages, or comments from TikTok end users.
  • No Commercial Transfer or Sale: Any data or identifiers received from TikTok APIs are never sold, transferred to third-party ad networks, or used for surveillance.
04

Local Storage of Application Credentials

API tokens (such as OAuth access tokens and client secrets) are stored exclusively in isolated local configuration files (e.g., .env or local credential caches) maintained by the channel administrator.

These tokens are secured behind environment-level access controls and are excluded from all public repositories via .gitignore rules to prevent unauthorized exposure.

05

Data Retention & Revocation of Permissions

Because we do not store personal data on remote servers, there is no personal database to maintain or monetize.

  • Revoking TikTok Permissions: You can revoke Silicon Verdict’s authorization at any time by navigating to your TikTok account settings: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions and removing Silicon Verdict.
  • Revoking Google / YouTube Permissions: You can manage or revoke access via the Google Security Permissions page.
  • Data Deletion Requests: If you wish to confirm the deletion of any local authentication tokens or session cache, you may contact us at imnotchego@gmail.com, and all local tokens will be immediately purged within 24 hours.
06

Children's Privacy

Our application and content are intended for general PC hardware enthusiasts and developers. We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

07

Security Safeguards

All API interactions with TikTok and YouTube are transmitted exclusively through modern, secure, encrypted channels utilizing Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3).

08

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain alignment with TikTok for Developers guidelines or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
